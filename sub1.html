  • 1909
    FOUNDED IN MOLSHEIM
    Ettore Bugatti opens his own factory in Molsheim, Alsace.

  • 1947
    ETTORE BUGATTI DIES
    The founder passes away and the company slowly loses its way.

  • 1963
    SOLD TO HISPANO-SUIZA
    The Bugatti family sells the company and the Molsheim plant.

  • 1987
    REVIVED
    Italian entrepreneur Romano Artioli buys the brand and founds Bugatti Automobili.

  • 1991
    CAMPOGALLIANO
    A new factory near Modena opens, and the EB110 brings Bugatti back to life.

  • 1998
    BACK TO MOLSHEIM
    The Volkswagen Group acquires the brand and returns it home to Molsheim.

  • 2005
    THE ATELIER OPENS
    A new atelier is inaugurated next to the Château Saint Jean.

  • 2021
    BUGATTI RIMAC
    Bugatti and Rimac join forces in a new company.

03 - THE ATELIER

MADE
BY HAND

Every Bugatti is assembled by hand from more than 1,800 parts in the Atelier in Molsheim - no robots, no conveyor belt.

  • 01
    POWERTRAIN
    The 16-cylinder engine and gearbox are joined into a single unit at the first station.
  • 02
    CHASSIS & BODY
    Across 12 stations - no robots, no conveyor belt - the car is built entirely by hand.
  • 03
    PAINT & INTERIOR
    Every coat of paint is applied, sanded and polished by hand before the next.
  • 04
    TEST DRIVE
    300 km through the Vosges to Colmar, with high-speed runs on the airfield runway.
