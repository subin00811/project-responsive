ABOUT

— LA MARQUE

Pur Sang. Since 1909.

1909
Bugatti was founded in 1909 by Ettore Bugatti in Molsheim, Alsace. Born into a family of artists in Milan, Ettore believed that a car should be as beautiful as it is fast — a work of art built around an engine.
This single principle shaped every car that left Molsheim, from the legendary Type 35 — winner of more than 2,000 races — to the record-breaking Veyron and Chiron.
  • “If comparable,
    it is no longer Bugatti.”
    - ETTORE BUGATTI
    More than a century later, every Bugatti is still assembled by hand in the Atelier in Molsheim. Art, Forme, Technique: three words that have guided the brand for 117 years.
